This is what a short, informal note looks like: a title, a date, some
tags, and Markdown content below. Inline math like $E = mc^2$ or
$\pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ renders in the flow of a sentence, and
display equations get their own line:
$$
\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s}, \qquad \mathrm{Re}(s) > 1.
$$

New posts go in `content/blog/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`; the list page at
`/blog/` sorts by date automatically.
