To a positive braid word $\beta$ on $n$ strands one can attach a braid
variety $X(\beta)$, parametrizing certain flags compatible with the
crossings of $\beta$. These varieties are conjectured (and in cases known)
to be related to augmentation varieties of the Legendrian links obtained
as closures of $\beta$, and carry a cluster structure coming from the
combinatorics of the braid word. I am interested in the symplectic
geometry of these varieties and how it interacts with the mirror
symmetry picture above.
