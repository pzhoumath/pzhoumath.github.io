Categorification replaces the structure constants of a quantum group
$U_q(\mathfrak{g})$ with categories and functors between them, so that
relations such as
$$
E_i F_j - F_j E_i = \delta_{ij}\, \frac{K_i - K_i^{-1}}{q - q^{-1}}
$$
become isomorphisms of functors rather than numerical identities. I am
interested in the interaction between such 2-representations and
Lagrangian Floer theory, particularly where braid group actions on
Fukaya categories give geometric models for categorified braid group
actions.
