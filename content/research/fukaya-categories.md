A Fukaya category $\mathrm{Fuk}(X)$ is an $A_\infty$-category whose objects
are Lagrangian submanifolds of a symplectic manifold $X$, with morphism
spaces given by Floer cochain complexes and higher products
$$
\mu^k : CF^*(L_0, L_1) \otimes \cdots \otimes CF^*(L_{k-1}, L_k)
\longrightarrow CF^*(L_0, L_k)
$$
defined by counting pseudo-holomorphic polygons with boundary on the
$L_i$. I am interested in explicit models of Fukaya categories for
specific classes of spaces (e.g. Weinstein manifolds arising in
representation theory) where these counts can be made combinatorial.
