Kontsevich's homological mirror symmetry (HMS) conjecture predicts, for a
mirror pair $(X, \check{X})$, an equivalence of triangulated categories
$$
D^\pi \mathrm{Fuk}(X) \;\simeq\; D^b \mathrm{Coh}(\check{X}),
$$
between the (split-closed derived) Fukaya category of $X$ and the bounded
derived category of coherent sheaves on $\check X$. A guiding question in
my work is how this equivalence interacts with extra structure — group
actions, singularities, or a categorified symmetry — on either side.
