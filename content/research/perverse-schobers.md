A perverse schober is, roughly, a categorification of a perverse sheaf: on
a stratified space $S = \bigsqcup_\alpha S_\alpha$, instead of assigning
vector spaces (stalks) to strata with gluing maps between them, one
assigns categories $\mathcal{C}_\alpha$ with functors playing the role of
nearby/vanishing cycle functors. These arise naturally as Fukaya-type
invariants of Lefschetz fibrations, and I am interested in how local
models of perverse schobers near a singular fiber constrain the global
Fukaya category of the total space.
